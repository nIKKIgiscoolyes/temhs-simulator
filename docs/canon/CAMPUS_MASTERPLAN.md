# 8. CAMPUS SCALE

TEMHS contains:

# 32 TOTAL LEVELS

Established level structure:

- Floor 2
- Floor 1
- Level -1
- Level -2
- Level -3
- Level -4
- continuing downward
- Level -30

The campus therefore has:

- two modern above-ground floors;
- approximately thirty subterranean levels.

The fully explorable version covered by this project is restricted to:

# Floor 2
# Floor 1
# Level -1
# Level -2
# Level -3
# Level -4

Do NOT fully build Levels -5 through -30 yet.

However, those levels must remain physically and institutionally real.

Their existence should appear through:

- elevator panels;
- directories;
- signage;
- inaccessible stair flights;
- freight elevators;
- mechanical shafts;
- building maps;
- utility references;
- staff dialogue;
- course schedules;
- archive references;
- access-control displays;
- distant elevator activity;
- restricted doors;
- ventilation architecture.

The player should constantly understand:

> there is dramatically more TEMHS beneath Level -4.

By September 2026, roughly 25 underground levels have some student-facing or institutional function, although that does not mean every space is unrestricted or continuously accessible.

Do not use this fact as justification to build -5 through -30 in the current production scope.

# 9. PHYSICAL SCALE TARGET

The campus must be physically large, not merely described as large.

World scale:

# 1 BABYLON WORLD UNIT = APPROXIMATELY 1 METER

Typical walking speed:

approximately 1.3–1.5 m/s.

A major cross-level route between distant destinations should sometimes require approximately:

# 5–8 REAL MINUTES OF WALKING

before accounting for elevators, stairs, queues, or Passage congestion.

A major floor should have long routes of several hundred meters.

Do not create:

`elevator -> hallway -> six rooms -> stairwell`

and call it massive.

Each playable floor must contain:

- several major wings;
- minor wings;
- major junctions;
- secondary intersections;
- classrooms;
- specialized rooms;
- staff areas;
- bathrooms;
- service spaces;
- vertical transportation;
- mechanical interfaces;
- storage;
- wayfinding;
- circulation landmarks.

Use streaming rather than shrinking TEMHS.

---


# 10. MAP DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

TEMHS is not a perfect rectangular grid.

It has accumulated history.

Use:

- offset wings;
- corridors that change width;
- curved sections;
- long straight institutional corridors;
- widened intersections;
- ramps;
- old/new junctions;
- awkward structural columns;
- half-level connections where appropriate;
- corridors built around infrastructure;
- repurposed rooms;
- different ceiling heights;
- different renovation eras;
- service corridors behind public corridors;
- multiple stair systems;
- multiple elevator banks.

Players should gradually learn the campus by landmarks.

TEMHS must have a persistent master layout.

Never randomly regenerate the floorplan between play sessions.

Procedural techniques may populate or construct from the fixed map definition.

The map itself is canonical and stable.

# 58. ROOM DATABASE

Every meaningful room needs a persistent record.

Record:

- ID;
- floor;
- wing;
- room number;
- room type;
- subject;
- capacity;
- access class;
- architectural era;
- TMAP state;
- current class;
- current teacher;
- connected corridor;
- nearby exits.

Do not assign random numbers independently.

If canonical room-number rules are not available, create a documented implementation convention.

Legacy signage can preserve codes resembling:

`C-214`

while internal IDs remain unambiguous.

---


# 59. WAYFINDING

Because TEMHS is enormous, navigation infrastructure is essential.

Include:

- wall maps;
- floor maps;
- overhead signage;
- electronic directories;
- wing identifiers;
- level signs;
- elevator directories;
- construction detours;
- color-coded routing where appropriate;
- room plaques.

Example:

ARCHIVAL INSTRUCTION →
NORTH ACADEMIC →
CENTRAL LIFT BANK ←

Signs must correspond to the actual map.

Never generate fake wayfinding that points to nonexistent locations.

## Alpha 0.2 implementation note (non-canon dimensions)

The current fixed playable plan contains six levels and five instructional districts per level. Central, North, East, West and North Extension contain 180 total instructional rooms; 24 support rooms sit beside the cross-concourse. The footprint reaches about 430 metres northward, with roughly 220 metres east–west extent. These dimensions, room codes and repeated floor kits are implementation choices, not a replacement for institutional canon. Levels -5 through -30 remain outside the playable area.
